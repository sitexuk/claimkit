Using ClaimKit

ClaimKit helps you keep receipts and prepare expense claims. The details it reads from a receipt are suggestions made by software and can be wrong. You are responsible for checking every amount, date and VAT figure before you save or submit a claim. ClaimKit is not tax or accounting advice.

Subscription

  • ClaimKit has a 3-day free trial of up to 5 receipts a day. After that, adding receipts needs a subscription at £5.99 a month (the price shown in the app at purchase is the price that applies).
  • Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation of purchase. The subscription renews automatically each month unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • Manage or cancel in Settings › your name › Subscriptions on your iPhone. A free trial that is not cancelled becomes a paid subscription.
  • Without a subscription you can still view, search, export and back up everything you saved.
  • Use Restore purchases in the app to restore a subscription on a new device.

Fair use

The AI reading is limited per device. Do not try to get around those limits, or use the service in a way that harms it or others.

Your data

Your receipts are yours and stay on your phone. Please keep backups; we cannot recover receipts that are lost with a phone or a deleted app. See the privacy policy.

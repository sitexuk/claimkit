Common questions

Where are my receipts stored?

Only on your iPhone, inside the app. There is no ClaimKit account. If you delete the app, the receipts go with it, so use Settings › Backup & restore to keep a copy somewhere safe such as iCloud Drive.

How do I back up and restore?

Open Settings, choose Backup & restore, and share the backup file to Files or iCloud Drive. To restore on a new phone, install ClaimKit, open Settings › Backup & restore and choose the file.

How do I make an expense pack?

Put receipts into a trip, open the trip and use its export option. You get a ZIP containing the receipt images, a PDF summary and a CSV spreadsheet.

The app read something wrongly.

Receipts vary a lot, so please check the amounts before you save. Values marked “check” are the ones the app is less sure about. If a receipt is read badly and you are happy to share what it says, email us the text shown under View text on receipt (not the photo) and we will improve the reading.

How do I turn AI reading off?

Settings › AI reading. With it off, receipts are read only on your phone and nothing is sent anywhere.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: on your iPhone open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose ClaimKit and tap Cancel. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for. You can also request a refund from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I changed phone and my subscription is missing.

Open the subscription screen in ClaimKit and tap Restore purchases.

Delete my data

Everything you saved is on your phone: delete the receipts, or delete the app. The only thing on our server is a random device number used to count AI readings and prove requests come from the real app. ClaimKit does not collect your name or email, so we cannot tell which number is yours, but if you have a question about it, email us.
